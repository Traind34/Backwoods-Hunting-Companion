# Backwoods Development Build

Development branch is the active workshop. Main remains the stable production baseline.

Current suite areas: Dashboard, Property & Stands, Hunt Journal, Camera Center, Deer & Sightings, Conditions, Season History, Reports, Hunt Intelligence, Backup & Sync.

Cloud accounts, cellular camera ingestion, and multi-device sync require a backend service and are intentionally represented as the next infrastructure layer rather than falsely claiming they are live.
